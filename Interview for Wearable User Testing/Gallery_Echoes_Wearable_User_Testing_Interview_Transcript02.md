# Gallery Echoes — Wearable User Testing Interview Transcript 2

## Interview Introduction

**Interviewer:** Hello, thank you for joining our interview. Can I record our interview?

**Participant:** Sure.

---

## Question 1 — First Impression

**Interviewer:** What do you think this wearable device is designed to do?

**Participant:** I think it is supposed to give me extra information or social responses about the artwork.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** What was confusing about this device?

**Participant:** I think the connection between choosing a color and creating an Echo was not immediately clear to me.

**Interviewer:** So you think it was not completely clear to you?

**Participant:** Yeah.

---

## Question 2 — Wearability

**Interviewer:** How did the wearable device feel when you were wearing it?

**Participant:** It was okay for a short time, but I could still feel that something was on my wrist.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Would you wear it for a full gallery visit?

**Participant:** Maybe, but only if it was smaller and lighter.

**Interviewer:** So, more wearable or suitable?

**Participant:** Yeah.

---

## Question 3 — Palm Interaction

**Interviewer:** How natural did the palm interaction feel?

**Participant:** It was interesting, but it took me some time to understand what I was supposed to do.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Which part seemed unclear?

**Participant:** Looking at other Echoes was clear, but I was less sure about what the colors represented.

**Interviewer:** Do you prefer the palm interaction or using a phone?

**Participant:** I like the palm idea, but for more detailed information, I would still prefer a phone.

---

## Question 4 — Attention to the Artwork

**Interviewer:** Did the wearable help you stay focused on the artwork, or did it distract you?

**Participant:** A little of both. It made me look again, but I was also thinking about how to use the interface.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Was the vibration helpful?

**Participant:** Yes, but only if it happens at important moments. Too many vibrations would be annoying.

**Interviewer:** How long should the interaction take?

**Participant:** Maybe about 20 or 30 seconds.

---

## Question 5 — Echoes and Social Awareness

**Interviewer:** How do you think other visitors' Echoes affected your experience?

**Participant:** It was interesting, but I would prefer to form my own opinion before seeing other people's Echoes.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Did they influence your interpretation?

**Participant:** Yes. If I see other people's responses too early, I might start looking for what they noticed instead of forming my own view.

---

## Question 6 — Choice and Control

**Interviewer:** Did you feel that you had enough control?

**Participant:** Mostly, but I would like clearer options to skip interactions.

---

## Question 7 — Overall Experience

**Interviewer:** If this device were available in a real gallery, would you use it?

**Participant:** Yes, I would probably try it, but I would want to control how often it interacts with me.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** What was the most useful feature?

**Participant:** Seeing another visitor's perspective was the most interesting part.

**Interviewer:** What would you want to improve?

**Participant:** I would simplify the interaction and make the notifications less frequent.

---

## Closing

**Interviewer:** Okay, thank you very much.

**Participant:** Thank you.
