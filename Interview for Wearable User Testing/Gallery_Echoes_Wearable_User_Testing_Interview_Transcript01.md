# Gallery Echoes — Wearable User Testing Interview Transcript

## Interview Introduction

**Interviewer:** Hello, I want to do a test. Can I record this video?

**Participant:** Yeah, sure.

---

## Question 1 — First Impression

**Interviewer:** What do you think this wearable device is designed to do?

**Participant:** I think it is designed to help visitors interact with artworks and see how other people respond to them.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Was its purpose clear to you at first?

**Participant:** Mostly yes. The vibration made me feel that the device wanted me to interact with something nearby.

---

## Question 2 — Wearability

**Interviewer:** How did the wearable device feel when you were wearing it?

**Participant:** Well, at first, it felt comfortable and quite light. I did not really notice it after wearing it for a while.

---

## Question 3 — Palm Interaction

**Interviewer:** Which part felt the most natural?

**Participant:** Yeah, you are right. Let me see. Choosing a color was the easiest part because it was quick and visual.

---

## Question 4 — Attention to the Artwork

**Interviewer:** Did the wearable help you stay focused on the artwork, or did it distract you?

**Participant:** Overall, it helped. The interaction was short, so I quickly went back to looking at the artwork.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Did the vibration feel helpful or annoying?

**Participant:** It was helpful, but I would not want it to happen too often.

---

## Question 5 — Echoes and Social Awareness

**Interviewer:** How did seeing other visitors affect your experience?

**Participant:** It made the artwork feel more social. I could see that other people had different reactions to the same artwork.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Did you notice something new?

**Participant:** Yes, one Echo made me look at the artwork from another angle.

---

## Question 6 — Choice and Control

**Interviewer:** Did you feel that you had enough control?

**Participant:** Yes, especially because I could choose whether I wanted to continue interacting or not.

---

## Question 7 — Overall Experience

**Interviewer:** If this device were available in a real gallery, would you use it?

**Participant:** Yes. I think it adds another layer to the gallery experience without requiring too much attention.

---

## Closing

**Interviewer:** Thank you very much.
