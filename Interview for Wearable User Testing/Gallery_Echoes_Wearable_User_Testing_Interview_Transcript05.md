# Gallery Echoes — Wearable User Testing Interview Transcript 5


## Interview Introduction

**Interviewer:** Hello, thanks for joining our test. Is it okay if I record this interview?

**Participant:** Yes.

---

## Question 1 — First Impression

**Interviewer:** What do you think this wearable device is designed to do?

**Participant:** I think it helps visitors express their own reaction to an artwork and then compare it with other visitors’ reactions.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Was the purpose clear to you at first?

**Participant:** Yes, after the first interaction. Before that, I was not completely sure what the vibration meant.

**Interviewer:** Was there anything that confused you?

**Participant:** I was not sure whether the Echo was supposed to represent an emotion, an opinion, or something else.

---

## Question 2 — Wearability

**Interviewer:** How did the wearable device feel when you were wearing it?

**Participant:** It felt comfortable enough for me.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Would you feel comfortable wearing it during a gallery visit?

**Participant:** Yes, as long as it stays lightweight.

**Interviewer:** Would you prefer it to be more visible or more subtle?

**Participant:** More subtle. I would not want other visitors to pay attention to the device.

---

## Question 3 — Palm Interaction

**Interviewer:** How natural did it feel to interact with the interface on your palm?

**Participant:** I liked it because it felt more connected to the physical space than looking at a phone.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Was it easy to choose a colour and view an Echo?

**Participant:** Yes. The colour selection was simple.

**Interviewer:** Which part felt the most natural?

**Participant:** Opening my hand after the vibration and making a quick selection.

**Interviewer:** Which part felt less clear?

**Participant:** I would need a little more feedback after making a selection, so I know that my Echo has been saved.

---

## Question 4 — Attention to the Artwork

**Interviewer:** Did the wearable help you stay focused on the artwork, or did it distract you?

**Participant:** It helped more than it distracted me because I did not need to take out my phone.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Did the vibration feel helpful?

**Participant:** Yes. It was subtle enough to get my attention.

**Interviewer:** Did the “Look Again” prompt help?

**Participant:** Yes. That was probably the strongest part because it brought the experience back to the artwork.

**Interviewer:** How long should an interaction take?

**Participant:** Around 10 to 15 seconds would be ideal.

---

## Question 5 — Echoes and Social Awareness

**Interviewer:** How did seeing other visitors’ Echoes affect your experience?

**Participant:** It made me feel connected to other visitors even though I did not know them.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Did the Echoes help you notice something new?

**Participant:** Yes. I noticed that another person had a very different reaction, which made me look again.

**Interviewer:** Would you want to see other visitors’ Echoes?

**Participant:** Yes, but I would prefer not to see popularity numbers or rankings.

**Interviewer:** Why?

**Participant:** Because I do not want the experience to feel like social media.

---

## Question 6 — Choice and Control

**Interviewer:** Did you feel that you had enough control while using the device?

**Participant:** Yes, but I think every interaction should be optional.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Is it important to be able to ignore the vibration?

**Participant:** Yes. I may not want to interact with every artwork.

**Interviewer:** Would you prefer the system to suggest Echoes automatically?

**Participant:** Maybe sometimes, but I would still want to decide whether to open them.

---

## Question 7 — Overall Experience

**Interviewer:** If this device were available in a real gallery, would you use it?

**Participant:** Yes. I think it would make the visit more interactive without feeling like I am using social media.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** What was the most useful feature?

**Participant:** The combination of seeing other perspectives and then being encouraged to look at the artwork again.

**Interviewer:** Was there anything unnecessary?

**Participant:** I do not think it needs too much extra information. The simple interaction is enough.

**Interviewer:** If you could improve one thing, what would it be?

**Participant:** I would add clearer feedback after each action, but keep the interface simple.

---

## Closing

**Interviewer:** Thank you very much.

**Participant:** Thank you.
