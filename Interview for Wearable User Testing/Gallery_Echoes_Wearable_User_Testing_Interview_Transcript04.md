# Gallery Echoes — Wearable User Testing Interview Transcript 4

## Interview Introduction

**Interviewer:** Hello, thank you for taking part in our user test. Can I record this interview?

**Participant:** Sure.

---

## Question 1 — First Impression

**Interviewer:** What do you think this wearable device is designed to do?

**Participant:** I think it gives visitors extra ways to respond to artworks and see other people’s reactions.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Was its purpose clear to you at first?

**Participant:** Not completely. I understood the wearable part, but I did not immediately understand why the palm interface was needed.

**Interviewer:** Without explanation, would you know what to do next?

**Participant:** Probably not at the first artwork. I would need one short instruction.

---

## Question 2 — Wearability

**Interviewer:** How did the wearable device feel when you were wearing it?

**Participant:** It was okay, but I was aware of it on my wrist the whole time.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Did it feel too large, heavy, or distracting?

**Participant:** It was not heavy, but it felt a little bulky.

**Interviewer:** Would you wear it during a full gallery visit?

**Participant:** Maybe for a short exhibition, but for a long visit I would want it to be smaller.

---

## Question 3 — Palm Interaction

**Interviewer:** How natural did it feel to interact with the interface on your palm?

**Participant:** It was interesting, but not completely natural.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Which part felt awkward or unclear?

**Participant:** Holding my hand open for the interface felt a little awkward.

**Interviewer:** Was choosing a colour easy to understand?

**Participant:** Yes, choosing a colour was easy, but I was unsure what happened between the colour and the Echo.

**Interviewer:** Would you prefer the palm interaction or using a phone?

**Participant:** I would prefer the palm for quick actions, but a phone for anything more detailed.

---

## Question 4 — Attention to the Artwork

**Interviewer:** Did the wearable help you stay focused on the artwork, or did it distract you?

**Participant:** It distracted me a little at first because I was learning how to use it.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Did the vibration feel helpful or annoying?

**Participant:** It was helpful as a signal, but I think it should only happen occasionally.

**Interviewer:** Did “Look Again” help you return to the artwork?

**Participant:** Yes. That part was useful because it directed my attention back to the artwork.

**Interviewer:** How long should an interaction take?

**Participant:** Less than 20 seconds.

---

## Question 5 — Echoes and Social Awareness

**Interviewer:** How did seeing other visitors’ Echoes affect your experience?

**Participant:** I liked seeing different responses, but I was also worried that they could influence my opinion too much.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** When would you prefer to see other people’s Echoes?

**Participant:** After I have already formed my own first impression.

**Interviewer:** Did other people’s Echoes change your interpretation?

**Participant:** Yes, sometimes. That can be useful, but I would not want it to happen too early.

**Interviewer:** Does this feel less stressful than direct conversation?

**Participant:** Yes, definitely.

---

## Question 6 — Choice and Control

**Interviewer:** Did you feel that you had enough control while using the device?

**Participant:** Mostly, but I think the skip option should be more obvious.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Would you like to turn off or ignore the vibration?

**Participant:** Yes, especially if I want a quieter visit.

**Interviewer:** Would you prefer automatic suggestions or user-controlled interaction?

**Participant:** User-controlled interaction.

---

## Question 7 — Overall Experience

**Interviewer:** If this device were available in a real gallery, would you use it?

**Participant:** I would try it, but I am not sure if I would use it for the whole visit.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** What was the most interesting feature?

**Participant:** Seeing different visitor perspectives.

**Interviewer:** What would you change?

**Participant:** I would reduce the number of notifications and make the palm interaction simpler.

---

## Closing

**Interviewer:** Thank you for your time.

**Participant:** Thank you.
