# Gallery Echoes — Wearable User Testing Questions

## 1. First Impression

**Main Question:**  
What do you think this wearable device is designed to do?

**Follow-up Questions:**  
- Was its purpose clear to you at first?
- Was there anything that confused you?
- Without explanation, would you know what to do next?

---

## 2. Wearability

**Main Question:**  
How did the wearable device feel when you were wearing it?

**Follow-up Questions:**  
- Did it feel comfortable?
- Did it feel too large, heavy, or distracting?
- Would you feel comfortable wearing it during a gallery visit?
- Would you prefer the device to be more visible or more subtle?

---

## 3. Palm Interaction

**Main Question:**  
How natural did it feel to interact with the interface on your palm?

**Follow-up Questions:**  
- Was it easy to understand how to choose a colour and view an Echo?
- Which part felt the most natural?
- Which part felt awkward or unclear?
- Would you prefer this interaction or using a phone? Why?

---

## 4. Attention to the Artwork

**Main Question:**  
Did the wearable help you stay focused on the artwork, or did it distract you?

**Follow-up Questions:**  
- Which part of the interaction distracted you the most?
- Did the vibration feel helpful or annoying?
- Did the “Look Again” prompt make you look at the artwork differently?
- How long do you think each interaction should take?

---

## 5. Echoes and Social Awareness

**Main Question:**  
How did seeing other visitors’ Echoes affect your experience?

**Follow-up Questions:**  
- Did the Echoes help you notice something new about the artwork?
- Would you want to see other visitors’ Echoes? Why or why not?
- Did other people’s Echoes influence your own interpretation?
- Does this feel less stressful than talking directly to strangers?

---

## 6. Choice and Control

**Main Question:**  
Did you feel that you had enough control while using the device?

**Follow-up Questions:**  
- Would you like to be able to skip or ignore an Echo?
- Is it important for you to be able to turn off or ignore the vibration?
- Would you prefer the system to give suggestions automatically, or would you rather choose when to interact?
- Did any part of the experience feel forced?

---

## 7. Overall Experience

**Main Question:**  
If this device were available in a real gallery, would you use it? Why or why not?

**Follow-up Questions:**  
- What was the most useful or interesting feature?
- Was there any feature you felt was unnecessary?
- What would you change about the experience?
- If you could improve only one thing, what would it be?
