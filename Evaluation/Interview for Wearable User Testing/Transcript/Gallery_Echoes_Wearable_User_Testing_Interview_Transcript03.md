# Gallery Echoes — Wearable User Testing Interview Transcript 3

## Interview Introduction

**Interviewer:** Hello, thank you for joining our interview. Can I record this interview?

**Participant:** Yes, that's fine.

---

## Question 1 — First Impression

**Interviewer:** What do you think this wearable device is designed to do?

**Participant:** I think it is designed to help people interact with artworks without using their phone too much.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Was the purpose clear to you at first?

**Participant:** Mostly yes. I understood that the vibration was trying to guide me, but I was not sure what would happen after I opened my hand.

**Interviewer:** Was there anything else that confused you?

**Participant:** The idea of the Echo was not completely clear before I tried it.

---

## Question 2 — Wearability

**Interviewer:** How did the wearable device feel when you were wearing it?

**Participant:** It felt quite light, but I think the shape could be smaller.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Would you wear it during a full gallery visit?

**Participant:** Yes, probably, if it was comfortable enough and did not move around on my wrist.

**Interviewer:** Would you prefer it to be more visible or more subtle?

**Participant:** More subtle. I would prefer the artwork to stay as the main focus.

---

## Question 3 — Palm Interaction

**Interviewer:** How natural did the palm interaction feel?

**Participant:** It felt unusual at first, but after one try it became easier.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Which part felt the most natural?

**Participant:** Choosing a colour was easy because it was simple and visual.

**Interviewer:** Which part felt less clear?

**Participant:** I was not sure what each colour meant, but I also liked that the colours were open to interpretation.

**Interviewer:** Would you prefer this interaction or using a phone?

**Participant:** For a short interaction, I prefer the palm. For detailed information, I would still use a phone.

---

## Question 4 — Attention to the Artwork

**Interviewer:** Did the wearable help you stay focused on the artwork, or did it distract you?

**Participant:** It mostly helped because the interaction was short.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Did the vibration feel helpful or annoying?

**Participant:** Helpful, but it should not happen too often.

**Interviewer:** Did the “Look Again” prompt make you look at the artwork differently?

**Participant:** Yes. It encouraged me to look at the artwork again instead of staying with the interface.

**Interviewer:** How long should each interaction take?

**Participant:** Around 15 to 20 seconds would be enough.

---

## Question 5 — Echoes and Social Awareness

**Interviewer:** How did seeing other visitors’ Echoes affect your experience?

**Participant:** It was interesting because I could see that people reacted differently to the same artwork.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Did the Echoes help you notice something new?

**Participant:** Yes, especially when another Echo made me think about a different part of the artwork.

**Interviewer:** Would you want to see other visitors’ Echoes?

**Participant:** Yes, but only after I have looked at the artwork myself first.

**Interviewer:** Does this feel less stressful than talking directly to strangers?

**Participant:** Yes. It feels social without requiring a conversation.

---

## Question 6 — Choice and Control

**Interviewer:** Did you feel that you had enough control while using the device?

**Participant:** Yes, but I would like a very clear way to skip an interaction.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** Is it important to be able to ignore the vibration?

**Participant:** Yes. Sometimes I may just want to look at the artwork without using the device.

**Interviewer:** Would you prefer automatic suggestions or choosing when to interact?

**Participant:** I would prefer to choose most of the time.

---

## Question 7 — Overall Experience

**Interviewer:** If this device were available in a real gallery, would you use it?

**Participant:** Yes, I would try it because it feels different from using a phone.

### Follow-up

**Interviewer:** What was the most useful feature?

**Participant:** Seeing another person’s perspective without directly talking to them.

**Interviewer:** What would you improve?

**Participant:** I would make the device smaller and explain the Echo concept more clearly at the beginning.

---

## Closing

**Interviewer:** Thank you very much.

**Participant:** Thank you.
