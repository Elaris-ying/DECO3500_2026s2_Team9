# Interview Grouping

This document groups the initial interview codes based on similar ideas and issues found in the prototype evaluation.

## G1 Personal Responses to Artworks

**Group Focus:** Visitors understand Gallery Echoes as a way to express their own feelings and responses to artworks while also seeing how other visitors respond.

### Included Codes

- **C01 Personal Reflection and Seeing Other Visitors' Responses**
- **C02 Focus on Personal Responses**

---

## G2 Interaction Flow and Clarity

**Group Focus:** Visitors generally understand the interaction flow, but some steps, labels, and instructions need to be clearer and simpler.

### Included Codes

- **C03 Clear Guidance and Next Steps**
- **C04 Unclear Labels**
- **C05 Overall Flow Makes Sense**
- **C06 Shorter and Simpler Flow**
- **C07 Easy Ways to Leave a Response**

---

## G3 Understanding the Collective Visual

**Group Focus:** Visitors understand the collective visual as a combination of individual responses, but they also want a clearer connection between their own contribution and the final visual.

### Included Codes

- **C08 Collective Visual and Personal Contribution**
- **C09 Collective Visual Needs Clear Meaning**

---

## G4 Social Connection Through Visitor Responses

**Group Focus:** Other visitors' responses can create social connection, introduce different perspectives, and support reflection without requiring direct interaction. However, social features should remain simple and low-pressure.

### Included Codes

- **C10 Social Connection Through Shared Responses**
- **C11 Different Views Support Reflection**
- **C12 Casual and Anonymous Sharing Reduces Pressure**
- **C13 Social Features Should Stay Simple**

---

## G5 Keeping the Experience Artwork-Focused

**Group Focus:** Digital interaction should support the physical artwork rather than distract from it. Visitors also want control over when they see other people's responses.

### Included Codes

- **C14 Digital Content Should Stay Lightweight**
- **C15 Visitors Want to Form Their Own View First**
- **C16 Some Visitors Prefer Seeing Others' Responses First**

---

## G6 Supporting the Gallery Visit

**Group Focus:** NFC, artwork information, and navigation features can support the gallery visit by providing quick access to information and helping visitors move through the space.

### Included Codes

- **C17 NFC Provides Quick Access**
- **C18 Artwork Information Helps Understanding**
- **C19 Map and Visit Tracking Support Navigation**
