User Testing Interview – Participant 1
Can you briefly describe what you think Gallery Echoes is for, in your own words?
Interviewee:
I think Gallery Echoes is an innovative way to help visitors understand artworks better. Sometimes galleries only show the artwork with very limited information, so it can be difficult to understand what the artist is trying to express. I think this system can provide more information and also include some interesting interactive features.
Was there any point during the experience where you were unsure what to do next? What happened?
Interviewee:
No, I did not really feel confused during the experience. I thought the interaction was convenient and clear, especially the information panel.
After tapping the NFC tag, did the steps from viewing the artwork to sharing your feelings make sense to you? Why or why not?
Interviewee:
Yes, I think the process made sense. It did not take much time because I could simply tap the NFC tag and quickly access information about the artwork. Compared with searching for information on a website or social media, this felt much more convenient.
What did you think the collective visual represented?
Interviewee:
I thought the collective visual represented a connection between different visitors. People can have different opinions about the same artwork, and seeing those different perspectives can be interesting. It can also help visitors discover new ideas from other people.
How did you feel about seeing and interacting with other visitors’ reflections and sketches, such as reading comments or liking sketches?
Interviewee:
I thought this was very interesting. I have seen galleries provide paper and pencils for visitors to make sketches, but normally those sketches are only personal and I cannot see what other visitors have created. Being able to see other people’s sketches could give me inspiration and help me think of new ideas.
If you were actually visiting a gallery, which feature would you be most likely to use again, and what would you change or remove?
Interviewee:
I would probably use the artwork information feature again. One thing I would change is to make the information appear automatically after tapping the artwork or NFC tag. I would prefer the description to pop up directly on the screen instead of having to enter another page or take extra steps.
