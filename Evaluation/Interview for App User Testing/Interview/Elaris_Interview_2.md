# Gallery Echoes Interview Transcript — Natural Version

**Interviewer:** Can you briefly describe what you think Gallery Echoes is for, in your own words?

**Participant:** I think it’s for leaving a quick reaction to an artwork and seeing what other people thought about the same one.

**Interviewer:** So it’s less about art history and more about personal feelings?

**Participant:** Yeah, more about your own response to it.

---

**Interviewer:** Was there any point during the experience where you were unsure what to do next?

**Participant:** A little bit between P1 and P2. After I tapped “Add a thought”, P2 came up with the time and another button. I wasn’t sure if I was meant to wait there or just keep going.

The “Share artwork” button on P4 was also a bit unclear. I thought it might mean sharing it outside the app.

**Interviewer:** Did that slow you down?

**Participant:** Not much. I still tapped it, but I hesitated for a second.

---

**Interviewer:** After tapping the NFC tag, did the steps make sense?

**Participant:** Mostly, yeah. I understood what I was supposed to do, but I think there were a few too many screens before I could actually leave a response.

**Interviewer:** How would you streamline it?

**Participant:** Maybe combine P1 and P2. Then you could see the artwork info and go straight to the reflection part.

---

**Interviewer:** What did you think the collective visual represented?

**Participant:** I thought each dot was another visitor’s mood or reaction. The colours probably showed different feelings.

**Interviewer:** Did that make you feel connected to other visitors?

**Participant:** A little, yeah. You can see that other people reacted to the same artwork, even if you don’t talk to them.

---

**Interviewer:** How did you feel about the reflections and sketches?

**Participant:** I liked them. They were short and easy to look at, and the sketches felt pretty casual.

**Interviewer:** Would you contribute a sketch yourself?

**Participant:** Probably, yeah. If other people are just doing quick sketches, I wouldn’t feel like mine had to look good.

---

**Interviewer:** Which feature would you use again?

**Participant:** Probably the map and visit tracker. It would help me remember which rooms I’d already been through.

**Interviewer:** What would you change or remove?

**Participant:** I’d merge P1 and P2, and I’d probably remove the photo option on P3.

**Interviewer:** Why?

**Participant:** If I’m already standing in front of the artwork, I’d rather look at that than spend more time on my phone.
