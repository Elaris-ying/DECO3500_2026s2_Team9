# Gallery Visitor Interview Transcript

**Participant Profile:** Parent visiting with family, mainly interested in spending time together rather than art itself.

**Interviewer:** Can you tell me about the last time you visited an art gallery?

**Participant:** Um... yeah, I went with my family. It was mostly because I wanted to take my kid somewhere a bit different on the weekend.

**Interviewer:** What did you do during the visit?

**Participant:** We just walked around the exhibitions, looked at the artworks, and talked about some of them. Honestly, most of the art just went way over my head, so I was pretty much just following my child around.

**Interviewer:** Did you do anything else?

**Participant:** We took a few photos, I guess, and had to stop and sit down a couple of times. Walking around for that long gets pretty tiring.

**Interviewer:** Can you remember a moment when something made you stop for longer or pay more attention?

**Participant:** Hmm... let me think. There was an area where people could sit down and draw. My child really wanted to try it, so we stayed there for quite a while.

**Interviewer:** Did that change how you looked at the artwork?

**Participant:** Yeah, actually. Before that I wasn't really paying much attention, but once my child started drawing, I started looking at it more carefully too. We talked about the colours and some of the little details.

**Interviewer:** When you visit with your family, does that affect the way you experience the gallery?

**Participant:** Definitely. I think being with my family is pretty much the main reason I enjoy going at all.

**Interviewer:** Can you give me an example?

**Participant:** Sometimes I don't find an artwork interesting at all, but then my child gets excited about it or asks me something, and I'll stop and look at it with them. If I was there alone, I'd probably just keep walking right past.

**Interviewer:** Can you think of a time when another visitor changed what you noticed or did?

**Participant:** Yeah. We saw some other people sitting and sketching, and my child noticed them before I did.

**Interviewer:** What happened after that?

**Participant:** My child immediately wanted to try it too, so we headed over to the drawing area. I probably wouldn't even have noticed it if other people weren't already doing it.

**Interviewer:** Are there times when you prefer sharing the experience with other people?

**Participant:** Yeah, definitely. Honestly, I don't think I'd ever go to a gallery by myself.

**Interviewer:** What do you enjoy about going with your family?

**Participant:** Mostly talking about what we're looking at. Sometimes my child really likes something that I think looks... well, kind of strange. So we end up having completely different opinions, which is actually pretty fun.

**Interviewer:** Thinking back on the visit, what made it enjoyable or tiring?

**Participant:** The best part was probably seeing my child actually engaged, especially when there was something hands-on to do instead of just walking around looking at artworks.

**Interviewer:** And what was tiring?

**Participant:** Um... mostly all the walking. Also, sometimes we weren't really sure where to go next. We kind of ended up doing loops through the same rooms because we couldn't tell if we'd already seen everything.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything you think would make the experience better?

**Participant:** Maybe more things for kids to actually do, and clearer signs showing where to go. I think that would make it a lot easier to enjoy without feeling tired or lost.
