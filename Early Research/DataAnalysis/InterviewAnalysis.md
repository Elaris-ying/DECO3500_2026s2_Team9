# Interview Pain Point Coding Index

This document contains 18 interview pain-point codes. Each source excerpt is assigned to one code for traceability.

After reviewing the interview transcripts, we found that gallery experiences were shaped by several recurring factors: social interaction, physical space, interpretation, participation, and the use of technology. Visitors were influenced by companions and other visitors, but they also wanted to maintain control over their own pace and attention. Physical conditions such as crowding, seating, and wayfinding also affected how long and how deeply they engaged with artworks.

## C01 Feeling Rushed or Constrained by Companions' Pace

**Number of excerpts:** 6

### Original Excerpts

> **Fu_1:** “Like, if my friend isn't really interested in one gallery or one section, then we, we probably just look around quickly and move on. We don't spend much time there.”

> **Tracy_1:** “Though sometimes we move faster than I would alone.”

> **Tracy_3:** “Because, you know, with another person there's always this feeling like... are they waiting for me? Should we move on? So, yeah, alone is probably better if I want to take my time.”

> **Yifan_1:** “With friends, I feel like I shouldn’t stay in one place too long.”

> **minngjie xi:** “If everyone else has already moved on and I’m still looking at something, I start thinking I should catch up.”

> **minngjie xi:** “I realised I was skipping some of the information and moving on earlier than I normally would because I didn’t want them to keep waiting.”

## C02 Physical Fatigue and Inadequate Seating Reduce Time and Comfort

**Number of excerpts:** 6

### Original Excerpts

> **Elaris_1:** “We also sat down for a bit because, well, after a few rooms we were getting pretty tired.”

> **Elaris_2:** “Yeah... the bench wasn't very comfortable. It didn't have a backrest, so after leaning forward for twenty minutes, my back and neck were getting pretty stiff.”

> **Elaris_3:** “Walking around for that long gets pretty tiring.”

> **Tracy_2:** “So, yeah... by the end I definitely wanted to sit down.”

> **Yifan_1:** “Maybe not enough seats. If I want to draw for ten minutes and there’s nowhere to sit, it gets a bit tiring.”

> **minngjie xi:** “We looked for somewhere to sit, but the available seating wasn’t always near the things we were interested in.”

## C03 Unclear Routes and Wayfinding Cause Repeated Movement and Uncertainty

**Number of excerpts:** 2

### Original Excerpts

> **Elaris_3:** “Also, sometimes we weren't really sure where to go next. We kind of ended up doing loops through the same rooms because we couldn't tell if we'd already seen everything.”

## C04 Crowding Makes Artworks Harder to Access and Shortens Engagement

**Number of excerpts:** 4

### Original Excerpts

> **Fu_2:** “The crowds were probably the tiring part. There were just, there were a lot of people after a while.”

> **Tracy_1:** “But some popular rooms got crowded, which felt a little bit uncomfortable, I think.”

> **Tracy_3:** “Sometimes I wanted to get closer to something, but there were too many people standing there. So, yeah, that part was a little uncomfortable.”

> **minngjie xi:** “If it looks really crowded, I don't want to squeeze in or stand behind people waiting. I might think I’ll come back later, but sometimes I probably forget.”
