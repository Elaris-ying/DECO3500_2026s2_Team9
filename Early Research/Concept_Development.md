# Concept Development

Gallery Echoes was developed as a continuous visitor participation process. Visitors first express their personal responses, which then become digital traces that other visitors can discover, interpret, and save.

The concept uses anonymous, non-verbal, and asynchronous interaction to reduce social pressure and preserve the quiet gallery atmosphere.

## 1. Capture a Response

Research showed that visitors want to participate actively and express their own observations and feelings about artworks.

Therefore, the system provides a simple way for visitors to leave a short response connected to a specific artwork.

## 2. Visual Footprint

A visitor’s response is transformed into a unique visual representation connected to the artwork.

The final visual form may include colours, shapes, lines, motion, or other abstract elements, depending on later design decisions and testing.

The purpose is to make personal interpretations visible and shareable. It does not claim to accurately identify or measure the visitor’s real emotions.

## 3. Shared Visitor Traces

A Visual Footprint can be anonymously attached to the related artwork as a Shared Visitor Trace.

This turns an individual response into shared content that other visitors can encounter. Anonymous and asynchronous interaction also reduces the pressure of speaking directly to strangers.

## 4. Discover Different Perspectives

Visitors can use these traces to discover different observations, feelings, and interpretations of the same artwork.

They can choose whether to open and explore a trace. The system does not present any visitor’s response as the correct interpretation or force people to follow the choices of others.

## 5. Gallery Map

As visitors explore different areas, the Gallery Map helps them understand their location and visit progress.

The map can show visited, unvisited, and temporarily skipped areas. It provides navigation support while preserving visitors’ freedom to explore at their own pace.

## 6. My Collection

Visitors can save favourite artworks, their own Visual Footprints, and traces created by other visitors.

These saved items form a personal record of the visit and support reflection and sharing after leaving the gallery.

## Concept Flow

```text
Visitor views an artwork
↓
Visitor captures a personal response
↓
The response becomes an abstract visual representation
↓
The visual becomes an anonymous visitor trace
↓
Other visitors discover different perspectives through the trace
↓
Selected artworks and traces are saved in My Collection
```
