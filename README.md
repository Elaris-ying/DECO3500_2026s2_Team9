## Communication Plan

- **Main communication channel:** WeChat will be used for daily communication, quick updates, task coordination, and meeting reminders.

- **Backup communication channels:** Email and phone will be used when a team member cannot be reached through WeChat or when formal communication is required.

- **Expected response time:** Team members are expected to respond within 48 hours. Urgent matters should be clearly marked in WeChat and followed up by phone if necessary.

- **Meeting schedule and format:** The team will primarily hold face-to-face meetings during or after scheduled classes. Additional online or in-person meetings may be arranged when required. Key decisions, assigned tasks, and deadlines will be recorded after each meeting.

- **Decision-making process:** The team will first discuss each issue and attempt to reach a shared agreement. If an agreement cannot be reached, the decision will be made through a majority vote. Each team member will have one vote.

- **GitHub work management:** GitHub will be used as the central platform for storing project files, tracking tasks, and documenting project progress. GitHub Issues will be used to create and assign tasks, while GitHub Projects will be used to track tasks under categories such as `To Do`, `In Progress`, and `Completed`. Team members will commit their work regularly with clear commit messages. Important project information, meeting outcomes, and decisions will be documented in the README or GitHub Wiki.

## Conflict Resolution Plan

### Definition of Conflict

For our team, a conflict may include:

- A team member repeatedly failing to respond within the agreed 48-hour response time;
- Missing meetings without prior notice or a reasonable explanation;
- Failing to complete assigned tasks by the agreed deadline;
- Submitting work that does not meet the agreed requirements or quality standard;
- Making significant project decisions without consulting the rest of the team;
- Repeatedly failing to update assigned tasks or progress on GitHub.

### Raising an Issue

When an issue occurs, the concerned team member should first contact the person privately through WeChat. The issue should be explained clearly and respectfully, with reference to the agreed task, deadline, or team expectation.

If the issue cannot be resolved through messages, the relevant members will arrange a private face-to-face discussion during or after class. They will identify the cause of the problem and agree on a practical solution, such as revising the deadline, redistributing part of the task, or providing additional support.

### Internal Resolution Process

1. The relevant members discuss the issue privately and agree on a solution and a new deadline where appropriate.
2. If the issue continues, it will be discussed with the whole team during the next meeting.
3. The team will record the agreed actions, responsibilities, and deadlines in the meeting notes or GitHub Issues.
4. If necessary, the team may redistribute tasks or make a decision through a majority vote.
5. Staff will only be contacted if repeated internal discussions and documented actions have not resolved the issue.

When staff support is required, the team contact will provide a factual summary of the issue, previous communication attempts, agreed actions, and relevant GitHub records.
