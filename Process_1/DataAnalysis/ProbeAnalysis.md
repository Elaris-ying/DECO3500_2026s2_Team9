# Probe Findings and Feature Connections

## 1. Capture a Response

### Probe Finding

Interactive activities received the highest number of responses.

### Feature Connection

Visitors can leave a short written response, observation, or personal feeling about an artwork. This increases active participation in the gallery experience.

---

## 2. Visual Footprint

### Probe Finding

Colours and visuals were among the strongest elements for attracting visitors’ attention.

### Feature Connection

The system transforms a visitor’s written response into an abstract visual pattern. This allows their personal interpretation to be presented visually.

---

## 3. Shared Visitor Traces

### Probe Finding

Ways to interact with others received a high number of responses.

### Feature Connection

Visitors can view anonymous visual traces left by other visitors through the mobile interface. This creates indirect and asynchronous social interaction.

---

## 4. Non-verbal Social Interaction

### Probe Finding

A quiet atmosphere was important to participants.

### Feature Connection

Visitors communicate through abstract visual traces rather than live chat or direct conversation. This reduces noise and limits disruption to the gallery atmosphere.

---

## 5. Discover Different Perspectives

### Probe Finding

Exploring with others received a relatively high number of responses.

### Feature Connection

Visitors can discover different artworks, observations, and interpretations through traces left by other people while keeping control over their own exploration.

---

## 6. Gallery Map

### Probe Finding

Participants showed a clear need for routes, maps, and navigation support.

### Feature Connection

The map can display visited, unvisited, and temporarily skipped gallery areas. This helps visitors understand where they have been and reduces repeated walking.

---

## 7. Personalised Route

### Probe Finding

Personalised gallery routes received a positive response.

### Feature Connection

The system can suggest routes based on visitors’ interests, saved artworks, previous activity, or available time. Route suggestions remain optional.

---

## 8. My Collection

### Probe Finding

Ways to save favourites received a high number of responses.

### Feature Connection

Visitors can save favourite artworks, their own visual footprints, and traces created by other visitors. They can review this content during or after the gallery visit.
