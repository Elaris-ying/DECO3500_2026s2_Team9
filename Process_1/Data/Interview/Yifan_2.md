**Interviewer:** Okay, so, can you tell me about the last time you went to an art gallery?

**Interviewee:** Umm… yeah, I think it was maybe last month? I went by myself after class.

**Interviewer:** Oh, by yourself?

**Interviewee:** Yeah. I didn’t really plan it, actually. I just had some time, so I went in and walked around for a bit.

**Interviewer:** What did you normally do while you were there? Like, did you take photos or anything?

**Interviewee:** A few, yeah. Not that many. I listened to some audio thing as well, but… honestly, I stopped after a while.

**Interviewer:** Why did you stop?

**Interviewee:** Hmm… I don’t know, it was a bit too much information. Like, after a few artworks I just wanted to look, not keep listening.

**Interviewer:** Was there anything that made you stop for longer?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, there was this really big video on the wall.

**Interviewer:** What made it interesting?

**Interviewee:** It was super bright and kind of strange, haha. I didn’t really understand it, but I stayed there for a while.

**Interviewer:** So you didn’t need to understand it to enjoy it?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, I guess. Sometimes if something looks weird enough, I just wanna see what happens next.

**Interviewer:** When you go with friends, is the experience different?

**Interviewee:** Definitely. I talk way more.

**Interviewer:** About the artworks?

**Interviewee:** Sometimes, yeah. But sometimes just random stuff, haha. With friends I probably move faster too.

**Interviewer:** And when you’re alone?

**Interviewee:** I can stay longer. Like, if I like one thing, I don’t need to worry someone is waiting for me.

**Interviewer:** Can you remember another visitor doing something that changed what you noticed?

**Interviewee:** Oh yeah. One person was taking a photo from really far away.

**Interviewer:** And you noticed that?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, so I was like, “Why are they standing there?” Then I went to the same spot and… actually, the artwork looked better from there.

**Interviewer:** So you kind of learned from watching them?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, basically.

**Interviewer:** Do you usually prefer going alone, then?

**Interviewee:** Umm… mostly, yeah.

**Interviewer:** Why?

**Interviewee:** It’s just more relaxed. But for interactive stuff… maybe with friends is better.

**Interviewer:** Because?

**Interviewee:** Because doing it alone can feel a bit awkward, you know? Like if everyone is watching you press something or move around, haha.

**Interviewer:** Anything you didn’t really like about the visit?

**Interviewee:** Too much text, probably.

**Interviewer:** The labels next to the artworks?

**Interviewee:** Yeah. Some of them are so long. I start reading, then after like three or four, I’m just… nope.

**Interviewer:** So shorter information might work better for you?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, or maybe let me choose if I want more detail. That would be better.
