**Interviewer:** So, did you come here by yourself today?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, I did. I actually prefer coming alone most of the time.

**Interviewer:** Why do you prefer that?

**Interviewee:** Umm… I can go at my own pace. If I like something, I can stay there for ten minutes or even longer. I don’t need to worry about someone waiting for me.

**Interviewer:** Are you quite interested in art generally?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, pretty interested. I wouldn’t say I know a lot about art, but I enjoy looking at it. Sometimes I also search the artist later if I really like something.

**Interviewer:** Was there anything today that really caught your attention?

**Interviewee:** Hmm… there was one digital projection upstairs. I stayed there for quite a while.

**Interviewer:** What made you stop?

**Interviewee:** The image kept changing, and the sound was kind of… immersive? I don’t know if that’s the right word, haha. But yeah, it made me want to keep watching.

**Interviewer:** When you’re looking at something, do you pay attention to what other visitors are doing?

**Interviewee:** Sometimes, yeah. Especially if it’s interactive. I’ll watch first before I try it.

**Interviewer:** Just to understand how it works?

**Interviewee:** Yeah. I don’t wanna be the first person pressing random things, haha.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything that usually makes you spend more time with an artwork?

**Interviewee:** Probably if there are small details to notice. Or if the work changes over time. Static things… sometimes I look for a few seconds and move on.

**Interviewer:** What makes a gallery visit enjoyable for you?

**Interviewee:** Hmm, having enough space, not too crowded, and not too much explanation everywhere. I like finding things by myself.

**Interviewer:** So you don’t always want a lot of information?

**Interviewee:** Yeah. Maybe just basic information first, and if I’m interested, I can choose to know more.
