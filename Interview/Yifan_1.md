**Interviewer:** So… can you remember the last time you went to an art gallery?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, umm… I think it was a few weeks ago. I went with one of my classmates.

**Interviewer:** What did you usually do there?

**Interviewee:** Mostly just looking around. I took some photos, and I also did a bit of sketching.

**Interviewer:** Oh, you sketch when you visit galleries?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, sometimes. Not like a proper drawing, just quick copies. If I like the shape or composition, I’ll draw it in my sketchbook.

**Interviewer:** Do you do that quite often?

**Interviewee:** Hmm… yeah, pretty often actually. It helps me look at the work more carefully.

**Interviewer:** Was there anything that made you stop for longer that day?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, there was one painting with a lot of figures in it. I stood there for quite a while.

**Interviewer:** Because you wanted to sketch it?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, kind of. I was trying to copy one small part, but then I started noticing more details, like the hand positions and the spacing between people.

**Interviewer:** So drawing made you notice more?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, definitely. If I just look, sometimes I move on really fast. But when I draw, I have to actually… you know, look properly.

**Interviewer:** When you go with friends, does that affect how you visit?

**Interviewee:** A little bit. If I’m sketching, they sometimes have to wait for me, haha.

**Interviewer:** Does that make you feel rushed?

**Interviewee:** Sometimes, yeah. I’ll be like, “Wait, just two more minutes.” But if they’re interested too, it’s fine.

**Interviewer:** Do you ever show them what you drew?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, sometimes. And they might point out something I missed.

**Interviewer:** What about other visitors? Do they affect what you notice?

**Interviewee:** Hmm… yeah. Like, if someone is standing really close to one artwork, I’ll look there too.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever copied what another visitor was doing?

**Interviewee:** Kind of, yeah. Once I saw someone sitting on the floor sketching, and I thought, “Oh, okay, maybe I can sit here too,” haha.

**Interviewer:** So seeing someone else sketching made you more comfortable doing it?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, exactly.

**Interviewer:** Do you prefer visiting alone or with other people?

**Interviewee:** For sketching, probably alone.

**Interviewer:** Why?

**Interviewee:** Because I can take my time. With friends, I feel like I shouldn’t stay in one place too long.

**Interviewer:** But there are also things you prefer doing with friends?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, interactive installations. Those are more fun with people. You can try things together and laugh about it.

**Interviewer:** Was there anything uncomfortable or annoying during the visit?

**Interviewee:** Umm… sometimes I’m not sure if I’m allowed to sketch.

**Interviewer:** Really?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, like some galleries say no photography, but sketching is not clear. So I kind of look around first to see if anyone else is doing it.

**Interviewer:** Anything else?

**Interviewee:** Not really. Maybe not enough seats. If I want to draw for ten minutes and there’s nowhere to sit, it gets a bit tiring.

**Interviewer:** So seating actually affects how long you stay with an artwork?

**Interviewee:** Yeah, for me, definitely.
