### 1. Can you tell me about the last time you visited an art gallery? Who were you with, and what did you do during the visit?
**Possible follow-up:** Did you take photos, sketch, talk, rest, or do anything else besides looking at artworks?

**Answer:**
The last time I visited an art exhibition was a few months ago with a friend. We went to a free art museum in the city. During the visit, we looked at different paintings and installations, took photos of some of the artworks, and also participated in an interactive “painting together” activity.

### 2. Can you remember a moment during the visit when something made you stop for longer or pay more attention? What were you doing at that moment?

**Answer:**
There were some artworks that I thought were exceptionally well painted, so I stopped and spent more time observing them closely. I paid particular attention to details such as the brushstrokes, the expressions of the figures, and other elements of the paintings that made the works stand out to me.

### 3. When you visit with friends or other people, how does being with them affect the way you experience the gallery? Can you give an example?

**Answer:**
Visiting with other people can affect how much time I spend in different parts of the gallery. If the person I am with is not interested in a particular gallery or section, we usually move on quite quickly and do not spend much time there. However, if they are especially interested in something, I am happy to stay longer with them and ask why they like it. Their interest can encourage me to pay more attention to something that I might otherwise have passed by.

### 4. Can you think of a time when another visitor’s behavior, comment, or activity changed what you did or noticed in the gallery?

**Answer:**
When my friend praises a particular painting, I usually make a point of looking at it more carefully to understand what they find interesting about it. I also tend to notice the behaviour of other visitors, especially when someone behaves disrespectfully towards the artworks. For example, I have noticed visitors interfering with or damaging installations, which immediately draws my attention and affects my experience of the exhibition.

### 5. Are there moments when you enjoy sharing the experience with other people, and moments when you prefer to explore the gallery by yourself? Why?

**Answer:**
I generally prefer visiting galleries with other people because there are usually things that we can discuss and share our opinions about. Visiting alone allows me to observe the artworks in greater detail and spend as much time as I want on each piece. However, after the visit, the experience can sometimes feel a little empty because there is no one there to immediately share my thoughts or reactions with.

### 6. Thinking back on the visit, was there anything that made the experience especially enjoyable, uncomfortable, tiring, or memorable?
**Possible follow-up:** Was it related to the space, people, activities, or something else?

**Answer:**
Some parts of the exhibition used completely dark spaces for projections. I found these areas uncomfortable because of the lack of light and the overall atmosphere of the space.
