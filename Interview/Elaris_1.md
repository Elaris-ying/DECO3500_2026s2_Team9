# Interview Response

## 1. Can you tell me about the last time you visited an art gallery? Who were you with, and what did you do during the visit?

I went with a friend from art school. We walked around, talked about some of the artworks, took photos, and I did a few quick sketches. We also sat down for a while because we got tired after walking through several rooms.

## 2. Can you remember a moment during the visit when something made you stop for longer or pay more attention? What were you doing at that moment?

There was a painting with really interesting colours, so I stopped and sketched it for a few minutes. When I started drawing, I noticed more details in the composition and brushwork than I did at first.

## 3. When you visit with friends or other people, how does being with them affect the way you experience the gallery? Can you give an example?

I usually notice more things when I go with friends because we point things out to each other. Sometimes I don't care much about an artwork at first, but after my friend explains what they noticed, I go back and look at it again.

## 4. Can you think of a time when another visitor's behaviour, comment, or activity changed what you did or noticed in the gallery?

I once saw someone sitting in front of a painting and sketching it for a long time. I had already walked past it, but seeing them spend so much time there made me curious, so I went back to look at it again.

## 5. Are there moments when you enjoy sharing the experience with other people, and moments when you prefer to explore the gallery by yourself? Why?

I like sharing when I find something interesting or want to discuss an artwork. But when I'm sketching or looking closely at something, I prefer having some quiet time by myself.

## 6. Thinking back on the visit, was there anything that made the experience especially enjoyable, uncomfortable, tiring, or memorable?

Talking and sketching made the visit more enjoyable. The tiring part was the gallery layout because the route wasn't very clear, so we sometimes walked through the same area more than once. Some rooms also had so many artworks that I didn't really know what to focus on.
