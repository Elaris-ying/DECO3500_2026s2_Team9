# Interview Transcript

**Interviewer:** Can you tell me about the last time you visited an art gallery?

**Participant:** Um... yeah, I went to one pretty recently. We mostly just walked around and looked at the exhibitions.

**Interviewer:** Who were you with?

**Participant:** I went with a friend from art school.

**Interviewer:** Did you do anything besides looking at the artworks?

**Participant:** Yeah... we talked about some of the works, took photos, and I did a few quick sketches. We also sat down for a bit because, well, after a few rooms we were getting pretty tired.

**Interviewer:** Can you remember a moment when something made you stop for longer or pay more attention?

**Participant:** Hmm... let me think. Yeah, there was this one painting with really interesting colours. I remember stopping there for quite a while.

**Interviewer:** What were you doing at that moment?

**Participant:** I started sketching it. And, uh... once I actually started drawing, I noticed a lot more details, like the composition and the brushwork.

**Interviewer:** So sketching made you look at it more carefully?

**Participant:** Yeah, definitely. I think if I was just walking past, I probably wouldn't have looked at it for that long.

**Interviewer:** When you visit with friends, does that change the way you experience the gallery?

**Participant:** Yeah... quite a lot, actually. We usually point things out to each other.

**Interviewer:** Can you give me an example?

**Participant:** Um... sometimes I just walk past something because I don't find it that interesting at first. But then my friend might notice something and talk about it, and I'm like, "Oh... I guess I didn't see that." Then I'll usually go back and take another look.

**Interviewer:** What about other visitors? Has another visitor ever changed what you noticed or did?

**Participant:** Yeah, actually. I remember seeing someone sitting in front of a painting and sketching it for a really long time.

**Interviewer:** What did you do?

**Participant:** I had already walked past it, but... I don't know, seeing them stay there for so long made me curious. So I kind of went back to look at it again.

**Interviewer:** So their behaviour almost worked like a recommendation?

**Participant:** Yeah... sort of. I didn't talk to them or anything, but it still made me think, "Maybe there's something here that I missed."

**Interviewer:** Are there times when you like sharing the experience with other people?

**Participant:** Yeah, definitely. Especially when I see something interesting or maybe something a bit strange, and I just want to talk about it.

**Interviewer:** And are there times when you prefer being alone?

**Participant:** Hmm... yeah. If I'm sketching or really looking closely at something, I usually don't want to talk that much. I just want some quiet time.

**Interviewer:** Thinking back on the visit, what made it enjoyable or tiring?

**Participant:** I think talking with my friend and sketching were probably the most enjoyable parts.

**Interviewer:** What about the tiring part?

**Participant:** Um... the layout was a bit frustrating. The route wasn't super clear, so sometimes we ended up walking through the same area again.

**Interviewer:** Anything else?

**Participant:** I had already walked past it, but... I don't know, seeing them stay there for so long made me curious. So I went back and had another look.
