

# User Testing Interview – Participant 2

### 1. What do you think Gallery Echoes is for?

**Participant:**
Um, I think it’s basically a way to interact with the artwork a bit more. Like, normally in a gallery I would just look at something and then move to the next one. But here you can leave your reaction and also see what other people thought about it. So it feels a bit more interactive.

### 2. Was there any point where you were unsure what to do next?

**Participant:**
Yeah, a little bit. Most of it was pretty clear, but at one point I wasn’t sure if I had to submit my response before I could continue.

I think maybe the next button could be more obvious. Like, make it bigger or highlight it somehow. Even a small progress bar could help, because then I know, okay, I’m on step two or step three.

### 3. Did the NFC interaction and overall process make sense?

**Participant:**
Yeah, the NFC part made sense. You tap it, then you get more information about the artwork, and after that you can leave a response.

I think that flow is pretty natural. Maybe the part where it changes from looking at the artwork information to writing your own reflection could be a little clearer. I didn’t get stuck, but I did pause for a second.

### 4. What did the collective visual represent to you?

**Participant:**
I saw it as everyone’s different reactions being put together.

I actually liked that because people can look at exactly the same artwork and have completely different feelings about it. So instead of just seeing my own answer, I can kind of see the overall response from everyone.

### 5. How did you feel about interacting with other visitors’ reflections and sketches?

**Participant:**
I liked it. Sometimes another person notices something that I didn’t notice at all, so it can make me look at the artwork again.

I also liked the sketch part. Especially being able to just like someone’s sketch instead of having to write a comment every time.

But I probably wouldn’t want too many social features. Like, if there are followers, profiles, lots of comments and things like that, then it starts feeling a bit too much like social media. I think keeping it simple works better for a gallery.

### 6. Which feature would you use again, and what would you change?

**Participant:**
I’d probably use the NFC feature again because it’s quick. I also liked seeing the collective visual.

I would keep the reflections and sketches, but maybe make the whole process slightly shorter. And just make it really obvious what I’m supposed to press next. I think if it takes too long, people might just skip it, especially when they’re walking around the gallery.
