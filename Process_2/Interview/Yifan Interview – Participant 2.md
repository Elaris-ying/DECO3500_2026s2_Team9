User Testing Feedback – Participant 2

1. What do you think Gallery Echoes is for?
Gallery Echoes allows visitors to respond to artworks and see how other people feel about them. It makes the gallery experience more interactive instead of simply viewing an artwork and moving on.

2. Was there any point where you were unsure what to do next?
Most of the experience was clear, but there were a few moments when the participant was unsure whether they needed to submit something before continuing. They suggested adding a clearer Continue button, progress indicator, highlight, or hover feedback.

3. Did the NFC interaction and overall process make sense?
Yes. The participant felt that tapping the NFC tag, viewing more information about the artwork, and then sharing a reaction created a natural sequence. However, the transition into the reflection activity could be made slightly clearer.

4. What did the collective visual represent?
The participant understood it as a combination of different visitors’ emotions and responses to the same artwork. They liked that it showed how people could experience the same artwork in very different ways.

5. How did you feel about interacting with other visitors’ reflections and sketches?
They enjoyed seeing other visitors’ reflections because these could introduce perspectives they had not considered before. They also liked the sketch feature, especially the ability to simply “like” a sketch without having to write a comment. However, they preferred the interaction to remain lightweight rather than becoming too similar to social media.

6. Which feature would you use again, and what would you change?
The participant would most likely use the NFC interaction and collective visual again because they add to the physical gallery experience without requiring much effort. They would keep the reflection and sketch features, but simplify some steps and make the next action clearer.
