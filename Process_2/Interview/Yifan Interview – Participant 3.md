User Testing Feedback – Participant 3

1. What do you think Gallery Echoes is for?
The participant thought Gallery Echoes is a way for visitors to share their thoughts and feelings about artworks. It also allows people to see how other visitors experienced the same artwork.

2. Was there any point where you were unsure what to do next?
At first, the participant was unsure about what would happen after tapping the NFC tag. The next screen made the process clearer, but they suggested adding a short introduction or instruction before moving on.

3. Did the NFC interaction and overall process make sense?
Yes, mostly. The participant felt the process was natural: first viewing the artwork, then writing a short response, and finally seeing how that response became part of the experience. However, they felt the process could be slightly shorter.

4. What did the collective visual represent?
The participant understood the collective visual as showing how different visitors felt about the same artwork. They described it as a kind of shared memory created from many individual responses.

5. How did you feel about interacting with other visitors’ reflections and sketches?
The participant liked seeing other people’s responses because they could provide different ways of understanding the artwork. They also said that other visitors might notice details they had missed. However, too many comments could become distracting.

6. Which feature would you use again, and what would you change?
The participant would most likely use the feature that allows them to leave a short reflection and view the collective visual. They suggested keeping the interaction simple, reducing unnecessary steps, and making comments optional so visitors can choose whether they want to see other people’s opinions.
